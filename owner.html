Hello, I’m

Shafiq Malik.

Founder of Shafiq hub · Independent builder

I created Shafiq hub as a place to explore ideas and turn them into useful technology. I’m interested in the small and large ways software, automation, and thoughtful digital tools can make everyday life easier and more organised.

My starting point is simple: look at something that feels unnecessarily complicated, understand what makes it difficult, and explore how it could work more clearly. The result might be a product, a small utility, an experiment, or a lesson worth sharing.

This website brings those things together. It is where I share what I build, what I’m exploring, and what I learn along the way.

What guides me

Make technology useful in everyday life.

01. Start with a real problem

Understand the task, the friction, and what a better outcome would look like.

02. Keep the experience clear

Prefer practical features, straightforward flows, and interfaces people can understand.

03. Share the process

Document experiments, useful discoveries, and lessons that may help someone else.
